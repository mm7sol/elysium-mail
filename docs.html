How Elysium Mail works

Elysium Mail is wallet mail for Elysium, Kinetiq's L2 for Hyperliquid. Your wallet address is your mailbox: anyone can write to you at 0x… or at your .els name, and every message is signed by the sender's wallet.

Concepts

  • Wallet = identity. No account, no password, no phone number. You sign in by signing one readable sentence with your wallet. The session lasts 24 hours and the signature can never move funds.
  • Name = address. A .els name (e.g. maya.els) resolves on-chain to a wallet address. People write to the name, the name resolves to the wallet.
  • Signed messages. Each message carries a personal_sign signature over its exact text, so authorship is provable and impersonation is impossible.

Sign in

Open the app and click Connect wallet & sign in. Any EIP-1193 wallet works (Hyperliquid Wallet, MetaMask, Rabby, and others). The app requests your accounts, then switches (or adds) the Elysium chain:

Identity & .els names

Your identity is your wallet address. If you own a .els name registered on ELSnames and set it as your primary name, Elysium Mail resolves it on-chain and shows it instead of the raw address.

Forward resolution (name → address)

registry.resolver(namehash("maya.els"))  → resolver address
resolver.addr(namehash("maya.els"))     → 0x9c4e…21af

Reverse resolution (address → name)

namehash("9c4e…21af.addr.reverse") → resolver → name()

Names are managed by ELSnames (@ELSnames), deployed on Elysium mainnet:

Composing mail

  • To accepts a wallet address (0x…) or a .els name. When you type a .els name, Elysium Mail resolves it on-chain and shows the target address under the field.
  • Subject, body as usual. The signature preview is computed over to + subject + body.
  • Send stores the message in the Sent folder and shows a toast with the resolved recipient.
  • Drafts, archive, trash, starred, search, reply and forward work as in a classic inbox.

Contacts

Add a contact by name (a .els name is resolved on-chain automatically) and address. Click Write to open a compose window pre-filled with the recipient.

Settings

Shows your connected wallet address and your resolved .els name. If you have none, a link takes you to ELSnames to register one. Notifications toggle is stored locally.

Architecture

  • Static site (HTML/CSS/JS), no backend, no build step. Served by Vercel.
  • Storage: messages, contacts, session and identity are stored in localStorage (keys prefixed elys_). This is a demo: there is no server-side mailbox yet.
  • On-chain reads: .els resolution goes directly to the Elysium RPC (rpc.elysium.kinetiq.xyz) via ethers.js v6, in the browser. No API key required.
  • Wallet: native EIP-1193 provider (window.ethereum). No Privy, no embedded wallets.

Current limitations

  
    
    
  • Messages are local. There is no server-side mailbox yet: messages, drafts and contacts live in localStorage. They are not synced across devices or delivered to other users.
    • 
    
  • Signature is simulated. The signature shown is a deterministic hash, not a real personal_sign. Real signing will be wired to the wallet.
    • 
    
  • Encryption is described, not implemented. Mailboxes are not yet AES-256-GCM encrypted at rest.
    • 
    
  • Buying a .els name happens on ELSnames, not inside this app.
