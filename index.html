Under the hood

Signed by the sender.
Encrypted for the recipient.

Each message carries a personal_sign signature over its exact text, so authorship is provable and impersonation is impossible. Mailboxes are stored AES-256-GCM encrypted in private storage and only unlock with a signature from the owning wallet.
